Entry for the [The Best Meal You Ever Had](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85) bounty on make.fun ($2, deadline 27 Nov 2026). The program writes exactly one sentence to `entry.txt`: the best meal the entrant ever had, one concrete remembered moment.

## The sentence

> At 2 a.m. on a plastic stool by the roadside, my mother's nasi goreng, dark with kecap and smoky from the wok, fried from yesterday's rice, tasted like the whole city had stayed up just to feed me.

## Why this sentence

One remembered plate, not general praise: a named dish, a named cook, a time, a place, a smell and a texture, and a detail only a real kitchen has (good fried rice is fried from yesterday's rice). The ending turns a cheap roadside plate into a city keeping vigil for one hungry person, an image that stays after a single reading.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (mode 0644), after checking it is 1-200 characters long and a single sentence. Set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to write somewhere else.
